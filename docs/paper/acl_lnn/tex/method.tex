\section{\sys: Cross-Stage Pruning}
\label{sec:design}

\sys is a cross-stage pruning framework.
The \emph{decision} is pre-kernel admission, taken on the controller before any kernel runs (\S\ref{sec:app}); uncertain candidates are deferred to a short decode-side \emph{probe} that refines the decision (\S\ref{sec:split}); a pruning-aware copy-on-write context tree manages the KV \emph{state} created by these decisions (\S\ref{sec:kvstate}).
\Cref{alg:app} is the per-fan-out implementation; Appendix~\ref{sec:pipeline} gives the multi-step pipeline.
\S\ref{sec:accounting} shows how the three combine in prefill and transfer volume.

\subsection{Pre-Kernel Admission}
\label{sec:app}

For each residual the controller observes $o_i=(m_i^{\Pin},m_i^{\Din},s_i)$, where $m_i^{\mathcal{X}}$ is the parent-aware resident prefix of \eqref{eq:mi} and $s_i=\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{pre}}(\rho_i)\in[0,1]$ is a lightweight model-free heuristic score computed from the residual text in $O(\ell_i)$ CPU time.
We refer to $s_i$ as the prefill-side score.
Our scorer combines repetition detection and syntactic checks (Appendix~\ref{sec:app-detail}); a learned scorer can replace it without changing the policy.

\begin{definition}[Admission action]
\label{def:action}
Given thresholds $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}\le\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$, residual $i$ receives the first matching action in
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:action}
a_i=
\begin{cases}
\Hit & m_i^{\Din}=\ell_i,\\
\Admit & i=0\ \text{or}\ s_i\ge\tau_{\mathrm{dec}},\\
\Reject & s_i<\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\\
\Probe & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\noindent
\Hit requires residency on the \emph{decode} instance, since a page resident only on $\Pin$ must still be transferred.
Because $i{=}0$ is caught by the first two cases, $a_0\in\{\Hit,\Admit\}$: the top-ranked residual can be neither rejected nor probed away.
Residuals in the uncertainty band $[\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\tau_{\mathrm{dec}})$ are probed.
Setting $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ empties the band and yields aggressive prefill-side rejection (``Blanket''); replacing \Probe by \Admit yields \emph{reject-only admission}.
Let $e_i{=}[\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{dec}}(y_i)\ge\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}]$ indicate that the probe $y_i$ of a probed residual is extended (\S\ref{sec:split}).
The action determines the per-residual costs
\begin{align}
W_i &=
\begin{cases}
0 & \Hit,\ \Reject,\\
\ell_i-m_i^{\Pin} & \Admit,\\
\ell_i-m_i^{\Pin}+e_iW_i^{\mathrm{ext}} & \Probe,
\end{cases}
\label{eq:work}\\
X_i &= (\ell_i-m_i^{\Din})\,[a_i\in\{\Admit,\Probe\}],
\label{eq:xfer}\\
d_i &=
\begin{cases}
0 & \Reject,\\
D & \Hit,\ \Admit,\\
t_0+e_i(D-t_0) & \Probe,
\end{cases}
\label{eq:dec}
\end{align}
where $D$ is the decode budget, $t_0$ the probe length, and $W_i^{\mathrm{ext}}$ the tokens recomputed when a probe is extended (\S\ref{sec:split}).
The trunk is aliased (\S\ref{sec:kvstate}) and contributes $W^{\mathrm{trunk}}{=}L-c_u^{\Pin}$ and $X^{\mathrm{trunk}}{=}L-c_u^{\Din}$ once per fan-out.
A decode-time pruner (ESC, Speculative Rejection, DPTS) may further shorten $d_i$ for any admitted or extended residual.

\subsection{Decode-Side Probe}
\label{sec:split}

A probed residual is prefilled, transferred, and decoded for $t_0$ tokens; the decode-side pruner then scores the generated prefix, $s'_i=\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{dec}}(y_i)$, and extends the residual to $D$ only if $s'_i\ge\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$.
The two thresholds read different text: $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$ reads the residual before generation, and $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ reads model output.
They are therefore calibrated separately.
A useful pair satisfies the design condition
\begin{gather}
\sup_{\rho\in\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{rej}}}s(\rho)<\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}\le\inf_{\rho\notin\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{rej}}}s(\rho), \label{eq:sep-pre}\\
\sup_{y\in\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{stop}}}s'(y)<\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}, \nonumber
\end{gather}
where $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{rej}}$ is the class of residuals that should never be prefilled (here, repetitive residuals) and $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{stop}}$ the class of probes that should not be extended.
\Cref{tab:scores} lists the scores of our heuristic scorers on the three residual classes of our workload; with $(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\tau_{\mathrm{dec}})=(0.15,0.45)$ both conditions hold.
Because the heuristics assign near-constant scores per class, this separation is specific to our workload; a learned scorer can replace them on natural agent traces (\S\ref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{Extension cost.}
If the probe's pages remain resident, extension continues decoding without recomputation and $W_i^{\mathrm{ext}}{=}0$.
Our prototype issues the extension as a continuation request; when the probe's pages have been evicted in the meantime, the engine recomputes them, so $0\le W_i^{\mathrm{ext}}\le\ell_i+t_0$.
This term is negligible at $k\le 8$ but substantial at $k{=}16$ in our runs (Appendix~\ref{sec:ablate-math}), and pinning probe pages until the extension decision is an engineering fix we leave to future work.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{\sys{} (one fan-out).
Actions follow \eqref{eq:action}; costs follow \eqref{eq:work}--\eqref{eq:dec}.}
\label{alg:app}
\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE trunk $x_u$; residuals $\{\rho_i\}$; residency on $\Pin$/$\Din$; $(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\tau_{\mathrm{dec}},t_0,D)$
\ENSURE spine $x_\star$; $(W,X,D^{+})$
\STATE
\STATE \textbf{Phase I: Admit} \hfill \(\triangleright\) decide before any kernel
\STATE $a_i\leftarrow\pi(m_i^{\Pin},m_i^{\Din},\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{pre}}(\rho_i))$ for all $i$
\STATE $\mathcal{A}\leftarrow\{i:a_i\in\{\Admit,\Probe\}\}$;\;
  $\mathcal{U}\leftarrow\{i:a_i{=}\Probe\}$
\STATE $\mathrm{Fork}(x_u,\mathcal{A})$ \hfill \(\triangleright\) alias trunk; residual pages only
\STATE
\STATE \textbf{Phase II: Prefill \& Ship} \hfill \(\triangleright\) pay only what $\Pin$/$\Din$ miss
\STATE $\mathrm{Prefill}_{\Pin}(x_u,\{\rho_i\}_{i\in\mathcal{A}})$ \hfill \(\triangleright\) $\Pin$-miss; trunk once
\STATE $\mathrm{Ship}(x_u,\{\rho_i\}_{i\in\mathcal{A}})$ \hfill \(\triangleright\) $\Din$-miss
\STATE
\STATE \textbf{Phase III: Probe \& Commit} \hfill \(\triangleright\) refine on $\Din$, keep the spine
\FORALL{$i\in\mathcal{U}$}
  \STATE $y_i\leftarrow\mathrm{Dec}(x_u\Vert\rho_i;\,t_0)$
  \IF{$\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{dec}}(y_i)<\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$}
    \STATE $\mathrm{Abort}(\rho_i)$;\; $\mathcal{A}\leftarrow\mathcal{A}\setminus\{i\}$
  \ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\STATE $\mathcal{S}\leftarrow\{i:a_i{=}\Hit\}\cup\mathcal{A}$
\STATE $D^{+}\leftarrow\mathrm{Prune}(\mathcal{S};D)$;\;
  $\mathrm{Commit}(x_\star)$;\;
  $\mathrm{Abort}(\mathcal{S}\setminus\{\star\})$
\STATE \textbf{return} $x_\star$,\ $W$,\ $X$,\ $D^{+}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Work and Transfer Accounting}
\label{sec:accounting}

We compare \sys with a baseline that serves the same fan-out without admission.

\begin{assumption}[Baseline]
\label{ass:baseline}
The baseline admits every residual, computes $W_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{trunk}}$ trunk tokens according to its cache regime (\S\ref{sec:regimes}), computes $\ell_i-m_i^{\Pin}$ tokens for residual $i$, and transfers at least the pages each request lacks on $\Din$, i.e., $X_{\mathrm{B}}\ge(L-c_u^{\Din})+\sum_i(\ell_i-m_i^{\Din})$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Accounting]
\label{prop:acct}
Fix a fan-out and a cache state, and let $\Rej{=}\{i:a_i{=}\Reject\}$, $\mathcal{H}{=}\{i:a_i{=}\Hit\}$, and $\mathcal{E}{=}\{i:a_i{=}\Probe,e_i{=}1\}$.
Under Assumption~\ref{ass:baseline},
\begin{align}
W_{\mathrm{B}}-W &= \Delta_{\mathrm{trunk}}+\Delta_{\mathrm{adm}}-\Delta_{\mathrm{ext}}, \label{eq:acct}\\
X_{\mathrm{B}}-X &\ge \textstyle\sum_{i\in\Rej}(\ell_i-m_i^{\Din}), \nonumber
\end{align}
with trunk-sharing term $\Delta_{\mathrm{trunk}}{=}W_{\mathrm{B}}^{\mathrm{trunk}}-(L-c_u^{\Pin})$, admission term $\Delta_{\mathrm{adm}}{=}\sum_{i\in\Rej\cup\mathcal{H}}(\ell_i-m_i^{\Pin})$, and probe overhead $\Delta_{\mathrm{ext}}{=}\sum_{i\in\mathcal{E}}W_i^{\mathrm{ext}}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Sum \eqref{eq:work} and \eqref{eq:xfer} over $i$ and add the trunk terms.
The baseline charges $\ell_i-m_i^{\Pin}$ for every $i$, while \sys charges it only for $i\in\mathcal{A}$ and adds $W_i^{\mathrm{ext}}$ for $i\in\mathcal{E}$; the residuals not in $\mathcal{A}$ are exactly $\Rej\cup\mathcal{H}$.
For transfer, \Hit residuals satisfy $\ell_i-m_i^{\Din}{=}0$, so the baseline lower bound exceeds \sys's transfer by the rejected residuals' decoder-missing pages.
\end{proof}

The decomposition makes explicit which savings depend on the cache regime.
$\Delta_{\mathrm{trunk}}$ is zero in regimes R2--R4 and positive only when concurrent siblings recompute the trunk (R1); $\Delta_{\mathrm{adm}}$ does not depend on the regime; $\Delta_{\mathrm{ext}}$ is a cost.
Decode-time pruners change only $D^{+}$: since they act on generated tokens of residuals that have already been prefilled, $W$ and $X$ do not depend on the pruner.
We decompose the stage-local work of a fan-out as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:tfan}
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{fan}}={}&C_{\mathrm{pre}}(W)+C_{\mathrm{transfer}}(X)+C_{\mathrm{dec}}(D^{+})\\
&+C_{\mathrm{CoW}}+C_{\mathrm{abort}},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where $C_{\mathrm{CoW}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{abort}}$ are page-table operations that alias metadata and do not copy trunk KV.
This expression is an accounting abstraction rather than a wall-clock predictor, since execution and transfer may overlap; we therefore measure end-to-end latency directly.

\subsection{Pruning-Aware KV State}
\label{sec:kvstate}

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{\input{fig/fig_tree}}
\caption{\textbf{Context tree.}
(a)~Each edge is one \texttt{fork}: the child aliases the parent and allocates only a residual ($+$r).
Live paths share the trunk; A1b1x and A3a2x also share $+$r$_A$.
All descendants of fork C are aborted at depth 2, so reclamation propagates up to C and stops at the root, which retains the live children A and B.
(b)~A shared layer is one physical page set with reference count greater than one; aborting a leaf frees only its residual.}
\label{fig:tree}
\end{figure*}

\sys keeps one copy-on-write context tree per session (Appendix~\ref{sec:state}, \Cref{fig:tree}).
\texttt{fork} aliases the parent page table without copying the trunk KV; only residual pages are newly allocated.
Shared pages are reclaimed only after their last live reference is released.
A rejected residual allocates nothing; a probe that is not extended frees its residual and probe pages; abort frees the residual pages of every uncommitted candidate, $b\ell_i$ bytes each.
On the next observation $x$, commit binds the child with the longest common prefix,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lcp}
v^\star=\arg\max_{v}\mathrm{LCP}(x,x_v),\qquad g_u\leftarrow g_u+1,
\end{equation}
publishes the full pages of $x_\star$ to the prefix index, and invalidates in-flight work tagged with an older generation $g_u$.
Speculatively prefilled pages are never sampled from, so the emitted tokens equal those of a baseline that prefills committed tokens only.
After commit the live occupancy is $M_{\mathrm{spine}}$, and a pool of $M_{\mathrm{pool}}$ bytes holds $\lfloor M_{\mathrm{pool}}/M_{\mathrm{spine}}\rfloor$ sessions instead of $\lfloor M_{\mathrm{pool}}/M_{\mathrm{APC}}\rfloor$ while a prefix cache still retains aborted residuals.

\paragraph{Cross-step coordination.}
The committed spine of step $t$ becomes the logical trunk of step $t{+}1$.
Its KV residency on the prefill and decode instances is restored according to the underlying KV synchronization policy.
In our evaluation, the trunk is warm before each fan-out, so the admission results isolate residual-side prefill and transfer savings rather than cross-step trunk synchronization.
Once the required trunk pages are resident, subsequent fan-outs can reuse them without recomputing the shared prefix.
A residual becomes a \Hit{} only when its parent-aware prefix is already resident on the decode instance.
Admission is re-evaluated at every step, so rejected branches never accumulate state across turns.
