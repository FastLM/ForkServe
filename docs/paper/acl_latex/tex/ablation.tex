\section{Full Ablation Tables}
\label{sec:ablation-full}

This appendix gives the full ablations behind \S\ref{sec:eval}.
All runs use Qwen3-8B ($\mathrm{tp}{=}1$, temperature $0$, prefix caching) on the branching mix of \S\ref{sec:eval}:
GSM8K with $n{=}16$, $D{=}512$, and MATH-500 (first $32$ problems of level $\ge4$) with $D{=}1024$.
EOS is ignored, so decode volume is determined by the policy.
We report computed prefill $W$ (prompt minus cache hits), decode tokens $D^{+}$, wall-clock $T_{\mathrm{e2e}}$ in seconds (trunk warm-up plus fan-out),
committed accuracy (comm.), and any-finished coverage (any).

\paragraph{Analysis.}
At $k\le16$, the three base hosts report identical $W$ and admit counts.
Admission changes only that column: $3k/4$ residuals are admitted at $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.15$
(live and illegal; loops rejected) and $k/2$ at $0.45$ (live only).
Relative $T_{\mathrm{e2e}}$ cuts under ESC${+}0.15$ grow with $k$:
$5\%$, $6\%$, $12\%$, and $17\%$ at $k{=}4,8,16,32$.
Because SR and DPTS already terminate loops during decoding, the same threshold yields smaller relative reductions
($6\%$ and $3\%$ at $k{=}16$).

(i)~Blanket rejection of every $s_i{<}\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ loses coverage: $20/32$ falls to $13/32$ at $k{=}16$.
(ii)~Rejecting only loops costs at most one problem at $k\le8$ but recovers only $16/32$ at $k{=}16$.

(v)~At $k{=}16$, probe extension recomputes evicted pages, so $W$ exceeds that of the full fan-out although $D^{+}$ and wall-clock time decrease; retaining probe pages until the extension decision would eliminate this term.

\subsection{Token Accounting Checks}
\label{sec:ablate-idents}

Across \Cref{tab:ablate-thresh,tab:ablate-prefill}, the measured prefill satisfies the predictions of \Cref{prop:acct} for a warm trunk:
at $k\le16$ the three hosts report identical $W$, and admission removes exactly the computed tokens of the rejected residuals.
The control-plane connector table of \Cref{tab:connector} extends to wider fan-outs with the same $25\%$ loop-only cut on a stock prefiller and the same alias$+$gate reduction to the miss of $\Keep$ ($2240$/$4800$/$9920$ tokens at $k{=}8/16/32$).
Because residual lengths differ across classes, the reductions are not fixed fractions of $W$:
\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
 & $k{=}4$ & $k{=}8$ & $k{=}16$ & $k{=}32$ \\
\midrule
$W$, no admission & $1320$ & $2624$ & $5008$ & $10528$ \\
$W$, $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.15$ & $1018$ ($0.77$) & $2020$ ($0.77$) & $3848$ ($0.77$) & $7968$ ($0.76$) \\
$W$, $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.45$ & $732$ ($0.55$) & $1448$ ($0.55$) & $2768$ ($0.55$) & $5408$ ($0.51$) \\

Loops therefore account for about $23\%$ of residual prefill and illegal residuals for a further $22\%$, although each class is a quarter of the residuals.
Combined rows never exceed the prefill-only $W$ at the same $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$.
