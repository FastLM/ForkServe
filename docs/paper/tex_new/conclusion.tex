\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The KV retained for aborted branches fundamentally limits the throughput and memory cost of agentic LLM serving.
A Tree-of-Thoughts fan-out shares one trunk, yet a prefix cache retains every residual after the first expand, and a disaggregated connector transfers those pages even when the harness will abort them.
We introduced \sys, which makes the branch a serving object: a copy-on-write context tree, a cascade that rejects a residual at the minimum-cost stage at which rejection is admissible, and a decode path that attends only the committed spine.

Our evaluation demonstrates a $0.72\times$ median peak KV on Qwen3-14B GSM8K at matched accuracy ($0.845$ vs.\ $0.844$), a $74.72\%$ cut in modeled fan-out prefill against that cache, and a $35.14\%$ increase in the concurrency a fixed pool can sustain.
On Qwen3-8B the same stack emits $0.31\times$ the GSM8K decode tokens of the prefix cache and finishes in $0.75\times$ the time at four GPUs.
Across six model families the GSM8K spine stays at $0.72$--$0.75\times$ the prefix-cache peak, and the ratio falls to $0.55\times$ when the fan-out grows from $k{=}2$ to $k{=}8$.
Contest traces at a $2048$-token budget keep the ratio in $0.75$--$0.81\times$ with end-to-end time matched to the cache, because the spine, not the hashed union, is what remains live.

\sys's key innovations include: (i)~a copy-on-write context tree that turns \texttt{fork} into an alias of the parent's KV pages and frees only the residual on abort, so live occupancy is the spine $b(L+\ell_\star)$; (ii)~advanced prefill pruning, a cascade of block-hash reuse, draft-score rejection, early abort, and a transfer gate that inserts only the miss tail of a residual that is kept; (iii)~longest-common-prefix commit, which keeps speculative tokens out of the sampler so the committed sequence matches a prefill of committed tokens only; and (iv)~a last-line answer stop together with a two-class scheduler that admits speculative prefill only from the slack left by committed requests.

Future directions include: draft scores that reject model-generated continuations rather than only illegal or looping residuals, answer stops that preserve accuracy on families where a finished line is ambiguous, closed-loop QPS sweeps that confirm the analytical concurrency gain under a live scheduler, full-set Game24 and HumanEval forests, deeper trees and $32$K-context windows, and multi-tenant admission with explicit latency margins on a shared frame pool.

\sys shows that branch cost is incurred only for the residual that is committed, and only at the minimum-cost stage in the rejection cascade.
Co-designing the context tree with prefill admission and decode termination yields a spine whose occupancy is determined by $k$ and residual length, independent of tensor-parallel width.
As agentic workloads scale, serving systems that fork by reference, prune before prefill, and terminate on a completed answer retain KV capacity on the committed path.
