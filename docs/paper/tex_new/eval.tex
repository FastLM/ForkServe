\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

We separate four measurements.
\S\ref{sec:eval-gpu} is a GPU forest on Qwen3-14B.
\S\ref{sec:eval-app} is a control-plane cost model of \app against APC, hash prefill, stock P/D, and \sys.
\S\ref{sec:eval-plus} is a GPU run of ForkServe+ on Qwen3-8B.
\S\ref{sec:eval-multi} repeats the forest on six model families.

\subsection{GPU forest}
\label{sec:eval-gpu}

\paragraph{Setup.}
Qwen3-14B, \texttt{bfloat16}, $\mathrm{tp}{=}2$ on two A100-SXM4 40GB GPUs.
GSM8K test ($n{=}1319$) is Tree-of-Thoughts with $k{=}4$ and a $512$-token decode budget, scored by the official \texttt{\#\#\#\#} extractor.
Requests run in batches of eight ($165$ batches).
Recompute disables the prefix cache.
APC hashes full blocks after tokens exist.
\sys forks the trunk by reference, prefills residuals, aborts non-winners, and decodes the committed path.

\paragraph{Storage, not expand.}
\Cref{tab:gsm} is the storage result and the latency gap \app is built to close.
Median peak KV is $0.72\times$ APC and $0.25\times$ recompute; accuracy stays within one point of both.
Fan-out does not follow the storage ordering: \sys is $140$\,ms against APC's $87$\,ms, because CoW and abort sit on the expand path while APC's hash hit does not.
End-to-end generation stays within $2\%$ of APC ($18.9$ vs.\ $18.6$ minutes).
The same peak ordering, $0.58$--$0.80\times$ APC, holds on seven math sets for Qwen3-14B, Qwen3-8B, and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B at $\mathrm{tp}{=}2$ and $\mathrm{tp}{=}4$.
Peak does not change when the tensor width doubles; it tracks the committed path, as \eqref{eq:mem} requires.

\subsection{Prefill pruning}
\label{sec:eval-app}

\paragraph{Setup.}
Control plane, no GPU.
Ten sessions, $k{=}4$, $L{=}256$, $\ell{=}32$.
Two of four residuals fail the draft score; the winner is index $0$.
Prefill time is $\ell\times 12\,\mu\mathrm{s}/\mathrm{token}$; P/D transfer is $2\,\mu\mathrm{s}/\mathrm{token}$.
APC and hash prefill publish blocks only after a request materializes them, so the first fan-out clones $k$ trunks.
Stock P/D uses that same prefill and then ships every live token.
\sys shares the trunk by pointer swap and prefills every residual.
\app adds hash skip, draft prune, early abort, lazy abort, and a connector that inserts only retained residuals.

\paragraph{Fan-out.}
\Cref{tab:app} is the comparison the GPU gap motivates, and \Cref{fig:eval-kv} shows the occupancy timeline of one session.
Hash prefill matches APC on the first expand: blocks are unpublished, so the hash walk does not remove the clone.
Stock P/D is slower than APC ($162$ vs.\ $139$\,ms) because it ships the full clone.
\sys already drops the clone ($3840$ prefill tokens, $47$\,ms) by aliasing the trunk.
\app goes further: $20$ low-score residuals are not prefilled, $T_{\mathrm{fan}}$ falls to $35.9$\,ms, and the connector ships $352$ tokens rather than $11520$.
Replay of the published winner is a full hash hit for APC, hash prefill, and \app (zero additional $\Work$).

\paragraph{Concurrency from measured KV.}
Substituting DeepSeek-R1 GSM8K peaks ($1563$ APC tokens, $1163$ \sys tokens) into an $8$\,GiB pool in \eqref{eq:theta} gives $37$ concurrent trees for APC and $50$ for \sys.
\Cref{fig:eval-slo} sweeps offered QPS: under a $1$\,s P99 \ttft{} constraint the same model admits $192$ QPS for APC and $256$ for the pruned tree, and saturated decode throughput rises from $23680$ to $32000$ tokens/s (\gain{$+35\%$}).
This uses measured $M$, not the control-plane peak of \Cref{tab:app}.

\paragraph{Decode stop is the GPU latency term.}
APC always emits $nD{=}40960$ tokens.
\sys already stops on EOS ($0.52\times$).
ForkServe+ with the marker list emits $0.33\times$ at $\mathrm{tp}{=}2$ and $0.31\times$ at $\mathrm{tp}{=}4$, and e2e falls to $0.78\times$ and $0.75\times$ APC.
Accuracy at four GPUs is $74/80$ vs.\ APC $75/80$.
Fan-out is still slower than APC ($1048$ vs.\ $438$\,ms at $\mathrm{tp}{=}4$): real GSM8K thoughts do not fail the draft heuristic (\texttt{pruned\_branches}${=}0$), so expand remains CoW of every residual.
The $75\%$ prefill cut of \Cref{tab:app} is the illegal-residual case; the GPU latency win on this forest is $D^{+}$.
Game24 and HumanEval almost never hit the stop list, so their e2e stays within $1\%$ of APC, while peak KV remains below APC ($0.62\times$ and $0.96\times$).

\paragraph{What transfers.}
\Cref{tab:multi,fig:eval-multi} separate storage from stop.
The $M_{\mathrm{spine}}/M_{\mathrm{APC}}$ gap is stable across families and tensor width: it depends on $k$ and residual length, not on the weights.
Game24 is lower ($0.57$--$0.62$) because trunks are short.
ForkServe accuracy tracks APC.
ForkServe+ stop is not portable: Llama-3-8B keeps $0.675$ at $0.26\times$ APC tokens; DeepSeek-R1 hits \texttt{</think>} and scores $0$.
Quality comparisons across families should use \sys without the stop list.

HumanEval wrap+recovery ($k{=}2$) keeps peak $0.90$ / $0.89$ / $0.95$ of APC on Llama-3-8B, Mistral-7B, and Qwen3-14B.
Mistral-7B pass@1 is $0.350$ ($14/40$) on recompute, APC, and \sys at both tensor widths.
