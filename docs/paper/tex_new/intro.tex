\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

LLM serving began as a single-turn problem: a prompt arrives, the engine prefills, and tokens are streamed~\cite{orca,vllm,sglang}.
Production agents---coding assistants~\cite{sweagent,openhands}, computer-use systems~\cite{webarena}, and multi-agent workflows~\cite{autogen,metagpt}---execute programs of LLM calls interleaved with tools~\cite{react,compoundai}.
Tree-of-Thoughts and MCTS expand sibling thoughts that share a prefix and diverge in a short residual~\cite{tot,hive}.
The serving unit is a \emph{branching execution}, not a request.

Prior systems raise the atom from a request to a program~\cite{autellix,thunderagent}, a session~\cite{continuum,mori}, or a workflow DAG~\cite{ayo,parrot,pythia}.
They share an implicit assumption: the next LLM call is a unique continuation of the current context.
After a tool call is emitted, the next prompt is a \emph{distribution} over branches the engine can see \emph{before} the observation returns:
known suffixes with $p{=}1$ (templates, wrappers), fan-out siblings that share a trunk of length $L$ and differ in residuals $\ell_i$, and observation residuals with $p{<}1$.
Current engines wait until the chosen branch is fully materialized.
Automatic prefix caching (APC)~\cite{vllm,sglang} discovers sharing only after tokens exist. Prefill--decode disaggregation~\cite{distserve,splitwise} isolates \ttft{} from inter-token latency, but the connector transfers every KV page the prefiller computed.
Once KV retention has removed trunk recompute~\cite{continuum,mori}, the remaining \ttft{} term is residual-suffix prefill.

\paragraph{This work.}
We present \sys, a serving system whose first-class object is the \emph{branch} (\Cref{fig:system}).
Three mechanisms are coupled.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(1) A forkable context tree with copy-on-write KV pages (\S\ref{sec:state}).}
Each session is a tree of committed and speculative nodes.
\texttt{fork} creates a child that inherits the parent's pages by reference; writes allocate residual blocks.
\texttt{abort} drops a subtree and decrements residual refcounts only, so live occupancy collapses to the spine $M_{\mathrm{spine}}=b(L+\ell_\star)$.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(2) Cost-ordered prefill pruning and transfer gating (\S\ref{sec:app}).}
On top of the tree, each residual $\rho_i$ receives one action $a_i\in\Act$ and a pair $(\Work_i,\Xfer_i)$ (\Cref{fig:system}).
A published prefix of length $m_i$ is retrieved: $m_i=\ell_i$ is $\mathrm{HS}$ ($\Work_i=0$) and $0{<}m_i{<}\ell_i$ is $\mathrm{HP}$ ($\Work_i=\ell_i-m_i$).
A miss ($m_i=0$) has no row in $H$; $\mathrm{DS}$ and $\mathrm{EA}$ reject on $s_i{<}\tau$ before or inside the first $\alpha\ell_i$ tokens, and $\mathrm{PF}$ charges $\ell_i$ on the trunk aliased at \texttt{fork}.
Index $0$ is forced into $\Keep$, so the connector inserts only that kept miss tail~\cite{distserve,splitwise}.
LCP commit binds the observed prompt; speculative tokens never enter the sampler.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{(3) The spine on the critical path (\S\ref{sec:schedule}).}
Decode slots are $C=\lfloor H/M_{\mathrm{spine}}\rfloor$.
On tick $t$ the budget splits as $B_t=B^c_t+B^s_t$: $B^c_t$ decodes $x_\star$ and $B^s_t$ prefills $\rho^{+}_{\star}$ in the same step.
A known suffix with $p(w){=}1$ and $\Pref(|w|)\le\Idle(u)$ is pinned during the tool pause; the observation arrives as the unmatched tail.

\S\ref{sec:eval} reports four measurements that are not interchangeable.
On Qwen3-14B GSM8K, \sys matches APC accuracy at $0.72\times$ median peak KV; fan-out is still slower than APC.
A control-plane study of \app then shows that hash skip, draft prune, and the P/D gate remove most of that prefill on residuals that fail the draft score, and that the measured KV gap raises SLO-feasible concurrency by $35.5\%$.
The GPU rule is narrower than that blanket skip: only a repeated loop is dropped before prefill, and any other low-score residual is probed and extended when the generated prefix clears the threshold (\Cref{tab:grow}).
On Qwen3-8B GSM8K, $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.15$ keeps $16/16$ any-finished at $k{=}8$ and $k{=}16$ while cutting computed prefill by $23\%$ and ESC time from $17.05$\,s to $14.14$\,s; $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.45$ is faster at $k{=}16$ but drops two questions at smaller fan-out (\Cref{tab:prefill-bar,tab:plugin}).
A Qwen3-8B GPU run of ForkServe+ shows the same KV ordering.
A six-model forest ($n{=}40$) keeps GSM8K peak KV at $0.72$--$0.75\times$ APC.
