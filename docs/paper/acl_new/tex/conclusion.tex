\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys keeps the prefill pool and the decode pool, and changes the pages that cross between them.
One action per residual sets both the kernel and the connector to the radix miss of the residuals that are kept.
A repeated loop never enters that kernel.
A low decode score runs a $t_0$-token probe and continues only when the prefix meets $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$.
The pair is $(0.15, 0.45)$.
The trunk is an alias, so the miss excludes $L$, and abort leaves the spine.

On Qwen3-8B the gate cuts GSM8K prefill by $23\%$ at matched any-finished accuracy, and the MATH-500 probe restores the $20/32$ a blanket drop loses, at $72.2$\,s against $104.1$\,s.
The committed spine is $0.72$--$0.75\times$ the prefix-cache peak.
