\section{Full Ablation Tables}
\label{sec:ablation-full}

This appendix expands the ablations of \S\ref{sec:eval}--\S\ref{sec:eval-ablate}
from the GPU logs under \texttt{logs/prune\_ablation\_*}.
All GSM8K rows use Qwen3-8B, \texttt{bfloat16}, temperature $0$, prefix caching on,
$\mathrm{tp}{=}1$, four A100-40GB GPUs, $n{=}16$, $D{=}512$, and the hopeless residual mix
(half live strategies, half loop / illegal text).
MATH-500 rows use the first $32$ problems at level $\ge 4$, $D{=}1024$, contest family.
Time $T_{\mathrm{e2e}}$ is trunk-warm plus fan-out, in seconds.
Prefill $\Work$ is \emph{computed} tokens (prompt minus cache hits).
Decode $D^{+}$ is emitted tokens.
Any-finished (any) counts problems with at least one full-budget match;
winner (win) counts problems whose committed spine matches.
Stopped later is the number of admitted residuals the decode host drops at its own cut.

\paragraph{Reading the matrix.}
At every $k$, the three base hosts report identical $\Work$ and identical admit counts.
The gate moves that column only: $|\Keep|{=}k/2{+}k/4$ at $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.15$
(live half plus illegal half; loops withheld) and $|\Keep|{=}k/2$ at $0.45$
(live half only).
Relative $T_{\mathrm{e2e}}$ cuts under ESC${+}0.15$ grow with $k$:
$(8.65{-}8.22)/8.65{=}5\%$,
$(10.84{-}10.19)/10.84{=}6\%$,
$(17.05{-}14.13)/17.05{=}17\%$.
SR and DPTS already stop loops later, so the same gate yields smaller relative cuts
($5\%$ and $3\%$ at $k{=}16$).

\paragraph{Takeaways.}
(i)~Blanket prefill drop of every $s{<}\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ is inadmissible: any-finished falls from $20/32$ to $13/32$ at $k{=}16$.
(ii)~Skipping only loops is safe on accuracy at small $k$ but under-recovers at $k{=}16$ ($16/32$).

(iv)~Probing the loops as well never extends them and only adds cost ($74.44$\,s at $k{=}16$).

\subsection{Token identity checks}
\label{sec:ablate-idents}

Across \Cref{tab:ablate-thresh,tab:ablate-prefill}, the following identities hold on the hopeless mix
and are what Prop.~\ref{prop:host} and Def.~\ref{def:action} predict:
\begin{align}
\Work_{\mathrm{base}}(k)
  &= \Work_{\mathrm{ESC}}(k)
   = \Work_{\mathrm{SR}}(k)
   = \Work_{\mathrm{DPTS}}(k), \nonumber\\
\Work(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.15,k)
  &= \tfrac{3}{4}\,\Work_{\mathrm{base}}(k)
     \quad(k\in\{4,8,16\}), \nonumber\\
\Work(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.45,k)
  &= \tfrac{1}{2}\,\Work_{\mathrm{base}}(k), \nonumber\\
|\Keep|(0.15,k) &= \tfrac{3k}{4},\qquad
|\Keep|(0.45,k) = \tfrac{k}{2}. \nonumber
\end{align}
Measured: $\Work_{\mathrm{base}}(16){=}5008$,
$\Work(0.15,16){=}3848$ ($-23.2\%$),
$\Work(0.45,16){=}2768$ ($-44.7\%$).
Combined rows never increase $\Work$ above the prefill-only column at the same $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$.
