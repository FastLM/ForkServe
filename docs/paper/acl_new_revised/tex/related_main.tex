\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Inference-time search and path pruning.}
Tree-of-Thoughts, self-consistency, and Monte Carlo tree search trade tokens for accuracy by expanding many partial solutions~\cite{tot,selfconsistency,mctsllm,cot}.
ESC, Speculative Rejection, and DPTS stop branches after an agreeing window, a reward horizon, or a mini-step~\cite{esc,specrej,dpts}; STOP organizes such signals by source and learnability and trains an internal futility token~\cite{stoploss}; EquivPruner merges semantically equivalent steps~\cite{equivpruner}.
All of these decide from generated text and are agnostic to where the KV cache was computed.
\sys is complementary: it adds a pre-kernel decision point and a probe that hosts these pruners unchanged, and a learned scorer such as STOP's could serve as $\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{dec}}$.

\paragraph{Prefix sharing and disaggregation.}
vLLM and SGLang share prefixes through block hashing and radix trees~\cite{vllm,sglang}, Orca and Sarathi schedule at iteration and chunk granularity~\cite{orca,sarathi}, and DistServe and Splitwise separate prefill from decode~\cite{distserve,splitwise}.
These mechanisms determine whether a trunk is shared and how KV moves, but they serve every request they receive; \sys decides which residuals become requests and which pages need to move, using residency on both instances.

\paragraph{Agentic serving.}
Agentix and Hermes schedule at program granularity~\cite{autellix,hermes}; ThunderAgent and SAGA optimize workflows~\cite{thunderagent,saga}; InferCept, Continuum, and MORI retain KV across tool calls~\cite{infercept,continuum,mori}; ConServe schedules disaggregated prefill and decode at conversation granularity from observed agent behavior~\cite{conserve}; Sutradhara overlaps tool execution with the prefill of a single tool-independent prefix~\cite{sutradhara}.
ForkKV is closest in mechanism: it shares a base KV cache copy-on-write across LoRA agents~\cite{forkkv}.
These systems place, schedule, or retain contexts that will be served; ConServe in particular disaggregates scheduling but not pruning.
\sys targets the complementary decision of which sibling continuations should be served at all, and at which PD stage that decision is made.
