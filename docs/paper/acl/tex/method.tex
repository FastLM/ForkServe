\section{\sys}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Preliminary}
\label{sec:prelim}

\paragraph{Agentic prompting.}
Let $x=[x_t]_{t=1}^{L}$ denote a committed trunk of length $L$, and let $\rho_0,\ldots,\rho_{k-1}$ denote $k$ residuals proposed from $x$.
Each candidate is the concatenation $z_i=x\circ\rho_i$.
After the step, a single spine $x\circ\rho_{\star}$ remains.
Let $p_{\theta}(\cdot\mid z_i)$ be the next-token distribution of an LLM parameterized by $\theta$.
The model is expected to produce a correct answer from at least one viable residual; unpromising residuals should contribute neither prefill nor transfer.

State-of-the-art LLMs adopt the Transformer architecture~\citep{transformer}.
Let $H_i$ denote the KV cache of $z_i$ after prefill.
For a continuation $y_i=[y_{i,t}]_{t=1}^{D}$ of budget $D$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:decode}
p_{\theta}(y_{i,t}\mid z_i,y_{i,<t})=p(y_{i,t}\mid H_i,y_{i,<t},\theta).
\end{equation}
$H_i$ is reused during decode.
On a copy-on-write tree, $x$ is aliased in $O(1)$ and only $\rho_i$ allocates new pages; live occupancy after abort is the spine.

\paragraph{Fan-out cost.}
We write $W$ for the tokens actually executed by the prefill kernel, $X$ for the KV transferred from a disaggregated prefill instance to a decode instance~\citep{distserve,splitwise}, and decode-to-cut for the prefix generated before a prune score exists.
On the systems in Figure~\ref{fig:problem}(a), all three quantities are incurred before abort.

\paragraph{Decode-time pruning.}
ESC, speculative rejection, and DPTS assign scores to tokens that are already resident~\citep{esc,specrej,dpts}.
We write $y_i$ for a continuation of $z_i$ under a host cut: ESC decodes to $D$ while an agreement window is open, speculative rejection scores at a partial-reward horizon, and DPTS generates a mini-step.
A residual is wasted if $H_i$ is computed and $y_i$ is later aborted.
Because $y_i$ exists only after $H_i$ is encoded, $W$ is invariant to the host (Proposition~\ref{prop:invariance}).

\subsection{Disaggregated Prefill and Decode Pruning}
\label{sec:refuse}

To reduce this waste, \sys decouples admission from continuation.
The inference process comprises three stages (Figure~\ref{fig:arch}): scoring residual text and forming an admission mask (\textbf{Admit}), encoding KV only for the admitted set (\textbf{Prefill}), and generating a short prefix whose score determines whether the remaining budget is spent (\textbf{Probe and Resume}).

\subsubsection{Stage I: Admission}
\label{sec:prefill-admit}

Let $s^{\mathrm{pre}}:\rho\mapsto[0,1]$ be a prefill score on residual text.
We score only $\rho_i$, not the trunk $x$, because unpromising content is proposed in the residual.
Details of $s^{\mathrm{pre}}$ are deferred to Section~\ref{sec:scores}.

The designated residual $\rho_0$ is retained whenever no sibling survives.
Admission is therefore
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:keep}
\mathrm{keep}_i=[\,i=0\ \lor\ s^{\mathrm{pre}}(\rho_i)\ge\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}\,].
\end{equation}
Only residuals with $\mathrm{keep}_i$ are queued for the kernel.
$W$ is the measured prefill of that set; a withheld residual contributes neither $W$ nor $X$.

Applying $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ as a prefill threshold would discard illegal text that can still solve once a prefix is observed.
The default policy therefore uses $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$ for admission and reserves $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ for continuation.
Raising the prefill threshold to $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ is a faster operating point that we measure, not the default.

\subsubsection{Stage II: Prefill and Disaggregated Insert}
\label{sec:prefill-enc}

Admitted residuals are encoded on the aliased trunk.
The trunk is one shared prefix, so $L$ is not replicated onto each residual, and prefix caching still applies inside the admitted set.
The gate determines who is submitted.
Baseline disaggregation forwards every computed page, which is the payload in \eqref{eq:xfer-pd}.
\sys inserts KV only for $\mathrm{Keep}=\{i:\mathrm{keep}_i\}$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:xfer}
X_i = W_i\,[i\in\mathrm{Keep}].
\end{equation}
A withheld loop contributes neither prefill nor transfer.
Admitted residuals that Stage~III later stops have already incurred prefill; they do not incur the remainder of $D$, and pages that were never queued do not cross the connector.
On the accounting model in Appendix~\ref{app:xfer}---ten sessions, $k{=}4$, one loop among four residuals---\eqref{eq:xfer} reduces the connector from $11520$ tokens to $8640$.

\subsubsection{Stage III: Probe and Resume}
\label{sec:decode-cont}

Decode length is assigned after admission.
A repeated loop is never probed.
$\rho_0$ and any residual whose planner prior already exceeds $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ decode to $D$.
Every other admitted residual generates $t_0$ tokens and is extended iff
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:extend}
\mathrm{extend}_i=[\,s^{\mathrm{dec}}(y_{i,1:t_0})\ge\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}\,].
\end{equation}
Algorithm~\ref{alg:grow} implements this rule.
When a decode-time host is present (Section~\ref{sec:hosts}), the host supplies the later cut in place of $t_0$.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Probe and resume.
Loops skip prefill.
$\rho_0$ decodes to $D$.}
\label{alg:grow}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require residuals $\rho_{0:k-1}$, budget $D$, probe $t_0$, thresholds $(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\tau_{\mathrm{dec}})$
\For{$i\gets 0$ \textbf{to} $k-1$}
  \If{$i\neq 0$ \textbf{and} $\rho_i$ is a repeated loop}
    \State skip \Comment{no kernel, $X_i{=}0$}
  \ElsIf{$i=0$ \textbf{or} prior of $\rho_i\ge\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$}
    \State prefill $\rho_i$; decode to $D$
  \Else
    \State prefill $\rho_i$; decode $t_0$ tokens
    \If{$s^{\mathrm{dec}}(y_{i,1:t_0})\ge\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$}
      \State extend from $t_0$ to $D$
    \EndIf
  \EndIf
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Scoring Functions}
\label{sec:scores}

Admission is guided by $s^{\mathrm{pre}}$ and continuation by $s^{\mathrm{dec}}$.
Both are deterministic functions of a string.
They do not call the model and do not share weights.

The prefill scorer inspects $\rho_i$.
A repeated span is a loop and falls below $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$.
A residual matching an illegal-math pattern, such as a run of stars or an undefined token, lies strictly between $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$ and $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$.
Any other non-empty residual receives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:prefill-score}
s^{\mathrm{pre}}(\rho_i)=0.55\,\min(1,2\alpha_i)+0.45\,\min(1,8\eta_i),
\end{equation}
where $\alpha_i$ is the alphanumeric fraction and $\eta_i$ is character-level uniqueness.
Viable strategy text exceeds both thresholds.

The decode scorer inspects a generated prefix $y_i$.
A repeated prefix stays below $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$.
An answer mark (\texttt{\#\#\#\#} or \texttt{\textbackslash boxed}) exceeds $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$.
Illegal text on a prefix also stays below $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$, which is why it cannot share $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$.
Otherwise
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:decode-score}
\begin{aligned}
s^{\mathrm{dec}}(y_i)={}&0.50\,\min(1,2\alpha)+0.30\,\min(1,8\eta)\\
&+0.20\,\min(1,20\delta+15\omega),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with $\delta$ the digit fraction and $\omega$ the fraction of arithmetic symbols.
A static planner prior used by the host-side ranker is a third mix ($0.35$ alphanumeric, $0.65$ uniqueness) and is never substituted for $s^{\mathrm{pre}}$.

The operating point is the ordered pair $(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\tau_{\mathrm{dec}})$ with $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}<\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ (Equation~\ref{eq:pair}).
On the prefill axis the loop lies below $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$ and illegal text above it, so admission drops loops and keeps illegal text.
On the decode axis illegal text lies below $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ and an answer mark above it, so a probe stops on illegal text and resumes once an answer mark appears.
Section~\ref{sec:eval} measures the effect of using a single threshold for both decisions.

\paragraph{Score gap.}
The same illegal span can be interpreted as a residual to encode or as a prefix to stop, depending on whether the score is read before or after generation.
We refer to this discrepancy as the \emph{score gap}.
Figure~\ref{fig:arch} plots the four characteristic points on two number lines.
Applying $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$ as a prefill threshold drops illegal residuals that can still solve once a prefix is visible; applying $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}$ as a decode threshold would extend an illegal prefix.
Motivated by the gap, continuation uses only a prefix of length $t_0$.
On MATH-500, $t_0{=}128$ is the setting that returns $k{=}16$ any-finished accuracy to the full fan-out.

\begin{proposition}[Host-invariant prefill]
\label{prop:invariance}
Let $\mathrm{Keep}=\{i:\mathrm{keep}_i\}$ be the admitted set of \eqref{eq:keep}.
For any decode-time host that scores only generated tokens, the prefill work $W$ equals the measured prefill of $\mathrm{Keep}$ and does not depend on the host.
\end{proposition}
The proof is in Appendix~\ref{app:invariance}.
Proposition~\ref{prop:invariance} explains the identical prefill column in Figure~\ref{fig:problem}(a) and Table~\ref{tab:hosts}.

\subsection{Compatibility with Decode-Time Hosts}
\label{sec:hosts}

ESC, speculative rejection, and DPTS remain the decode policy.
\sys changes only the set of residuals that reach them.
\begin{itemize}
  \item ESC decodes an admitted residual to $D$ while its agreement window is open.
    On the evaluation mix the window stays open, so an admitted residual runs to $D{=}512$.
  \item Speculative rejection scores at horizon $\tau{=}256$ and drops the lower fraction $\alpha{=}0.5$ of the admitted set.
  \item DPTS generates a mini-step of $100$ tokens and early-stops a child whose confidence is below $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$.
\end{itemize}
The pair $(\tau_{\mathrm{pre}},\tau_{\mathrm{dec}})$ is not retuned per host.
When the prefill threshold is raised to $\tau_{\mathrm{dec}}$, the admitted set is the viable half, the host-side drop count falls to zero, and the three hosts converge to the same decode length.
\sys is complementary to these hosts: it does not modify their scoring rules.
A batch-limited rule that ignores the score and retains a single residual is a different policy; Section~\ref{sec:eval} shows that it discards correct trajectories.
