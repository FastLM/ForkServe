\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\paragraph{Setup.}
All GPU runs use vLLM on A100-SXM4 40GB GPUs with \texttt{bfloat16} weights, temperature $0$, and APC enabled; hyperparameters, mix construction, and run-to-run variation are in Appendix~\ref{sec:eval-full}.
Prefill and decode are physically disaggregated with a warm trunk (regime $R_3$), so prefill differences reflect residual admission only.
Admission and probe results use Qwen3-8B on a \emph{branching mix} of valid, repetitive, and malformed residuals for GSM8K and MATH-500; spine retention uses the full GSM8K test set on Qwen3-14B.
We report computed prefill, wall-clock, peak KV, committed-answer accuracy, and any-finished coverage.

\paragraph{Prefill-Side Admission Complements Decode-Time Pruning.}
\Cref{tab:main} (GSM8K block) places one admission policy in front of three decode-time pruners.
Without admission, ESC, Speculative Rejection, and DPTS prefill the same $5008$ tokens; they differ only in $D^{+}$ ($131072$, $98304$, and $78336$ tokens) because each reads generated text.
Reject-only admission at $\tau_{\mathrm{pre}}{=}0.15$ rejects the four repetitive residuals before any kernel and reduces $W$ to $3848$ tokens ($-23\%$) for every pruner, as \Cref{prop:acct} predicts.
Committed accuracy is unchanged for every pruner and coverage remains $16/16$.
The time reduction is largest for ESC ($-12\%$), which would otherwise decode the repetitive residuals to the full budget, and small for pruners that already stop them early ($-6\%$, $-3\%$).
Aggressive prefill-side rejection (``Blanket'') at $0.45$ also removes the malformed residuals and is faster ($-34\%$ for ESC); at $k{=}16$ coverage stays $16/16$ and committed accuracy drops by at most one problem (DPTS).
\Cref{tab:prefill-bar} shows that the trade-off depends on width: at $k{=}4$ and $k{=}8$, Blanket loses one to two problems of coverage, while reject-only stays within one of the pruner-only baseline.
At $k{=}32$ the same reject-only gate cuts ESC wall-clock by $17\%$ and blanket by $49\%$, both still at $16/16$ coverage.

\paragraph{Decode-side probing mitigates the coverage loss of aggressive prefill-side rejection.}
On MATH-500 (\Cref{tab:main}, lower block), Blanket halves the time of the full fan-out but lowers coverage from $20/32$ to $13/32$: some residuals scored as malformed nevertheless lead to correct solutions once decoded.
Reject-only admission reaches $16/32$.
At $k{=}16$, probing the uncertainty band with $t_0{=}128$ increases coverage to $20/32$ at $72.2$\,s, $0.69\times$ the full fan-out, and reduces $D^{+}$ from $524288$ to $393216$ tokens.
Most uncertain residuals pass the decode-side refinement in this workload; Appendix~\ref{sec:ablate-math} provides the detailed probe outcomes and recomputation overhead.
The cost of probing is visible in $W$: at $k{=}16$, evicted probe pages are recomputed on extension and $W$ rises to $28736$ tokens, which \eqref{eq:acct} accounts for as $\Delta_{\mathrm{ext}}$; at $k\le8$ the overhead is below $300$ tokens.
The best probe length depends on width (\Cref{tab:grow}): $t_0{=}64$ is preferable at $k{=}8$, and at $k{=}4$ no probe matches the full fan-out.

\paragraph{Connector volume.}
\Cref{tab:connector} instantiates \Cref{prop:acct} for $10$ sessions with $k{=}4$, $L{=}256$, $\ell_i{=}32$, and a cold trunk ($\bar L_{\Pin}{=}\bar L_{\Din}{=}L$).
Stock P/D is request-local, $(\nu^{\Pin},\nu^{\Din}){=}(k,k)$, so both columns equal $10\bigl(kL+\sum_i\ell_i\bigr){=}11520$.
Withholding the repetitive residual without aliasing leaves three request-local survivors, $(\nu^{\Pin},\nu^{\Din}){=}(3,3)$, and both columns fall to $8640$.
Of that $2880$-token drop, $(4{-}3)\,L\times 10{=}2560$ is trunk-amplification reduction and $32\times 10{=}320$ is the rejected residual: the two terms of \eqref{eq:xacct}.
Alias$+$gate shares the trunk and, in this counter, ships only the miss tail of $\Keep$, $10\times 3\times 32{=}960$ tokens ($0.08\times$ stock P/D).
That column charges $X^{\mathrm{trunk}}{=}0$ rather than the single cold-trunk copy $\nu^{\Din}{=}1$ would transfer, so the $8640{\to}960$ gap is the whole request-local trunk, $3L$ per session, i.e.\ the trunk-transfer term of \eqref{eq:xacct} with nothing left to ship; the residual admission term does not change.
The same ordering holds at $k\in\{8,16,32\}$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:ablate-idents}).

\paragraph{Pruning-aware spine retention lowers post-selection KV occupancy.}
On the full GSM8K test set ($n{=}1319$, Qwen3-14B, $\mathrm{tp}{=}2$, $k{=}4$), retaining only the committed spine matches APC within one point of committed-answer accuracy ($0.845$ vs.\ $0.844$) and reduces median peak KV usage to $0.72\times$ that of APC ($1030$ vs.\ $1430$ tokens; Appendix~\ref{sec:eval-gpu}).
This result comes from state management alone (\cowonly, i.e., \sys without admission), since admission does not change the committed spine.
The ratio follows $(L+\ell_\star)/(L+\sum_i\ell_i)$ and is therefore governed by $k$ and the trunk-to-residual ratio rather than by the model: it is $0.72$--$0.75\times$ on GSM8K and $0.57$--$0.61\times$ on Game of 24 across six families (\Cref{fig:eval-multi} in Appendix~\ref{sec:eval-multi}), and it falls from about $0.90$ at $k{=}2$ to $0.53$--$0.55$ at $k{=}8$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:eval-sens}).
The copy-on-write fan-out incurs its own overhead: its median latency is $140$\,ms, compared with $87$\,ms for an APC hash hit.

\paragraph{Multi-step agent sessions.}
We run a three-step Tree-of-Thoughts session ($k{=}4$ per step, $n{=}16$, Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B, $\mathrm{tp}{=}2$) in which each step's spine is the next step's trunk (\Cref{tab:mt}).
Admission runs before every fan-out and prefills $48$ of the $192$ residuals.
Peak KV is $0.86\times$ APC on GSM8K and $0.82\times$ on Game of 24 for both \cowonly and \sys.
Committed accuracy matches APC on Qwen3-4B GSM8K, is one problem lower on Qwen3-8B GSM8K ($15$ vs.\ $16$ of $16$), and is one to three problems higher on Game of 24.
The answer-marker stop in the \sys column is an orthogonal optimization and is not attributed to \sys.
End-to-end times in \Cref{tab:mt} therefore mix admission with this stop, and we do not treat those reductions as a \sys result.
